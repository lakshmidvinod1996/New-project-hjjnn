Fed-batch CHO culture · 2,000 L stirred-tank · 14 days

Antibody Bioreactor

Chinese hamster ovary cells carrying the antibody's heavy- and light-chain genes grow in a stirred, sparged tank. Every cell secretes antibody into the medium, and over two weeks the broth builds up several grams per litre.

Where the bioreactor sits in manufacturing

  1. Day −21 to 0Seed trainA vial of cells is expanded through flasks and smaller reactors.
  2. Day 0 to 14Production bioreactorCells grow and secrete antibody. Shown above.
  3. Day 14HarvestCentrifuge and depth filters remove cells.
  4. DownstreamProtein A captureAntibody binds the resin by its Fc region; the rest washes away.
  5. DownstreamPolishingIon exchange, viral inactivation and filtration.
  6. FinalFillFormulated drug substance goes into vials.

Values follow a typical modern fed-batch run (temperature shift on day 5, bolus feeds on days 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11). They are illustrative, not data from a specific process. Cell and antibody counts in the drawing are scaled down; a real 2,000 L tank holds around 30 quadrillion cells at peak.
